**Spatial positions and envelopes remain assumed.** Source pipe sizes and connection patterns are recorded separately from geometry assumptions. The model covers domestic water; sanitary, vent and gas are not included. It is a working model study, not an as-built or a dimensionally verified owner presentation. See the review PDF's final page for the next required dimensions and unresolved connections.
