# Asset notes

- Building geometry and underground DWV coordination: project 25-048 bid documents and supplied route controls.
- Exterior appearance and photographic glazing samples: five owner-supplied reference photographs.
- All architectural, landscape, and detail geometry was generated for this visualization. No third-party model-library assets were used.
- Interactive viewing uses Three.js 0.180.0 (MIT License), bundled locally with no runtime CDN dependency.
- The downloadable bid set is the owner-supplied Exhibit A contract coordination reference.
- Coordination visualization only; not a measured façade survey, fabrication model, or surveyed as-built.
