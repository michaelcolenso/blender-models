# Known Uncertainties / Do Not Overstate

These are intentionally unresolved in v4 and should remain visibly tagged or treated as model assumptions until verified.

1. **Existing underground inverts and depths** — plan-traced/model-solved for coordination, not surveyed.
2. **Exact concealed legacy fitting type at each junction** — topology is modeled; wye/combo/tee classification is field verify where the drawings do not expose the fitting.
3. **B-101 prior ABS repair limits and exact transition fitting** — prior repair is known to remain, but its full concealed extents/transition are not fully documented.
4. **Final vertical cut elevations** — model targets use 36 inches / 10 pipe diameters as coordination logic; actual cut location must allow proper fittings, couplings, support, and code-compliant connection.
5. **Existing WC carrier manufacturer/model** — modeled generically; documents say existing wall-mounted toilets are of various manufacture.
6. **Kitchen P-trap demolition at retained-prior-repair boundary** — conditional until field access confirms what must be disconnected/replaced.
7. **Foundation/structural crossing details** — no unverified structural demolition should be inferred from visualization geometry.
8. **Vapor-barrier type/condition at each trench** — expose, identify, preserve, then transition/lap new system.
9. **Final demolition trench width / exact concrete quantity** — current geometry is a coordination model; field/as-built measurement controls quantity.
10. **Finish demolition extents** — allowance quantities and access strips are distinct. Do not inflate finish demolition geometry merely to equal allowance quantities.
11. **As-built fitting labels / dimensions** — final WP #3 documentation must be updated after installation/field verification.

If the Blender execution reveals geometry that appears more precise than these facts support, reduce the implied certainty rather than adding detail.
