The camera enters the east glazed Lobby 110 frontage, crosses its connector to Corridor 108, takes an illustrative elevator ride to R2, then follows Corridor 208 toward Stair M1. The evening presentation adds warm windows, canopy and corridor lighting, a glowing floor route, opening lobby and elevator doors, a continuous glass-and-brass elevator section, in-scene wayfinding and a phone mini-map. The video has a synthesized ambient score with elevator chimes. Nearby building footprints come from OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0); their alignment and heights are approximate. A2.03/A2.04 give these spaces and A3.11 gives the 10 ft 3 in floor rise. Interior wall positions are provisional; doors, lights, elevator details and finishes are illustrative. This is not a verified view of the occupied building. Rebuild with `bash projects/highland-drone/build.sh` from the repository root.
