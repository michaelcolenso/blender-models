The camera enters the east glazed Lobby 110 frontage, crosses its connector to Corridor 108, takes an illustrative elevator ride to R2, then follows Corridor 208 toward Stair M1. The evening presentation adds warm windows and canopy lights, a gold floor route, proximity-opening entry doors, a glass-and-brass elevator cutaway, and a synthesized ambient score with elevator chimes. A2.03/A2.04 give these spaces and A3.11 gives the 10 ft 3 in floor rise. Interior wall positions are provisional; doors, lights, elevator details and finishes are illustrative. This is not a verified view of the occupied building. Rebuild with `bash projects/highland-drone/build.sh` from the repository root.
