# Role & Objective
You are an expert 3D Technical Artist, Architectural Visualizer, and Blender Python (`bpy`) Developer. Your task is to build a highly structured, dimensionally accurate, and modular 3D model in Blender of **Building B (Ground Floor Units B-101, B-102, B-103, and B-104)** at the **101 Meydenbauer Condominiums** for an **Underground Drain-Waste (DW) Pipe Replacement Project**.

The primary goal of this model is **4D Construction Phasing Animation**. Every layer of the building—from sub-grade soil and under-slab piping up to concrete slabs, waterproofing membranes, drywall patches, custom tiles, and plumbing fixtures—must be modeled as separate, cleanly grouped, and animatable objects.

# 4. Animation Rigging & 4D Phasing Requirements
Set up the Blender timeline (or Scene View Layers / Geometry Nodes / Custom Properties) with **8 distinct animation phases** controlled by an Empty controller object named `PHASE_CONTROLLER` (with custom integer property `Construction_Phase` from 1 to 8):

1. **Phase 1: Existing Conditions & Borescope Investigation**
   * Semi-transparent X-ray shader on existing floors and slab showing corroded cast-iron under-slab piping (`Ex_Cast_Iron_Pipes_DEMO`), prior ABS repairs in B-101/B-104, and an animated borescope locator path moving West-to-East from unit toilets to the East side sewer main and South-to-North from Building C's cleanout.
2. **Phase 2: Site Staging, Containment & Fixture Removal**
   * Animate the exterior 1,000 sq. ft. fenced staging area along SE 3rd St, floor-to-ceiling dustproof partitions, and temporary bypass piping.
   * Animate the disconnection and lifting/sliding out of toilets (`WC-1`), bathtubs (`BT-1`), vanities (`LAV-1`), washer/dryers (`WB-1`), and kitchen base cabinets (`KS-1`) to protected storage.
3. **Phase 3: Selective Demolition, Slab Sawcutting & Excavation**
   * Animate removal of existing floor tiles/hardwood along trench paths and lower drywall cutouts (up to 10 pipe diameters high at the 12 vertical stacks).
   * Animate neat-line sawcutting of the 5"–6" concrete slab (`Concrete_Slab_Trench_Cuts_DEMO`), lifting out slab chunks, and excavating trenches 2.5' to 4.5' deep inside the units and 18" outside the East foundation wall.
   * Animate removal of corroded cast iron pipes while keeping vertical stacks supported above cuts.
4. **Phase 4: New Schedule 40 ABS Pipe Installation**
   * Animate placement of 4" gravel bedding, followed by the assembly of black Schedule 40 Solid Core ABS under-slab piping (sloped 1/4" per foot), P-traps, floor drains with trap primers, base-of-stack wyes, 4" cleanouts, and exterior 6" cleanouts (`Zurn Z-1450-6` with 12"x12" concrete pad).
   * Highlight UL-listed firestop sleeves and 2mm circular steel plates at wall/footing penetrations.
5. **Phase 5: Water Leak Testing (WP #2)**
   * Animate test plugs sealing system openings and a blue water shader filling the new ABS piping system (10-foot head of water / flood level test) to visually represent the City of Bellevue inspection prior to concealment.
6. **Phase 6: Trench Backfill, Vapor Barrier & Concrete Pourback**
   * Animate compacted granular pipe-zone fill (to 12" above pipe) and general backfill lifts.
   * Animate laying the 15-mil Stegowrap Class A vapor barrier lapped over existing membrane, drilling/epoxying #4 rebar dowels 4" into existing slab edges, placing #4 longitudinal rebar @ 12" O.C., and pouring 3000 psi concrete with Kryton KIM crystalline additive flush with adjacent slab.
7. **Phase 7: Wall Patching, Waterproofing & Custom Finish Restoration**
   * Animate 2x4 wood nailers, single/double-layer 5/8" Type X gypsum patches (UL U305 / U334) with staggered seams, red fire/acoustical sealant, Level 5 drywall finish, and corner-to-corner floor-to-ceiling semigloss latex painting.
   * Animate application of vapor-mitigating epoxy primer on the slab, 220V radiant heating coils (in B-102 and B-104), TCNA crack-isolation/waterproofing membranes, and the unit-specific porcelain, marble, and engineered hardwood flooring.
8. **Phase 8: Fixture Reinstallation & Final As-Built State (WP #3)**
   * Animate reinstallation of cabinets, quartz countertops, appliances, and plumbing fixtures (with toggleable options for Alternate #1 floor-mounted toilets and Alternate #2 new bathtubs).
   * Include a final "As-Built Callout Overlay" layer showing dimension lines (+/- 2" accuracy) and fitting labels.

# 5. Deliverable Format Instructions
1. Provide a clean, modular **Blender Python (`bpy`) script** (or step-by-step procedural script modules) that generates the collection hierarchy, materials, grid lines, architectural walls/slabs, trench boolean cutters, pipe curves/meshes, and keyframed phase visibility drivers.
2. Use **non-destructive modeling**:
   * Use `Bevel`-extruded Bezier/Poly Curves for all piping (`Ex_Cast_Iron`, `New_ABS`, `Side_Sewer`) with accurate outer diameters so slopes and routing can be adjusted easily.
   * Use **Boolean Modifiers** on the concrete slab and drywall meshes driven by the `Trench_Volume` and `Wall_Cutout` meshes so cuts can be animated smoothly.
   * Set up an **X-Ray / Cutaway Shader Node Group** on the structural walls and floors so the camera can see underground trenches and in-wall stacks clearly from an isometric or 3/4 perspective view.
