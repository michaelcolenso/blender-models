## Bottom line

The 700 SF slab allowance (Allowance No. 7) matches the drawn routes at the detailed 2'-0" trench,
with almost no margin. The quantity risk is **depth**, and the documents leave depth open.

| Case (all four units) | Slab cut | vs 700 SF | Excavation (bank) |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| S1: 2'-0" trench only (10/P5.1) | 590 SF | −110 | 72 CY @ 3.5 ft POC invert |
| S2: S1 + 3'×3' pit at each of 30 fixture/stack connections | **686 SF** | **−14** | **82 CY** (103 CY loose) |
| S3 @ 4.5 ft POC invert: 4'-0" wide wherever trench ≥ 4'-0" | 991 SF | **+291** | 158 CY |

- **312.7 LF** of new under-slab ABS: 105 LF of 2", 33 LF of 3", 175 LF of 4". The building
  drain is ≈56% of the run. B-104 is the largest unit (96.5 LF, 215 SF of slab in S2).
- POC inverts are not documented ("MATCH EX. I.E."). The SOR's recorded depths are 2½–4½ ft. If
  the invert at a POC is deeper than about **3.7 ft**, the trench there is ≥ 4 ft deep. At 4.5 ft,
  294 of 313 LF is ≥ 4 ft. Trench protection then widens the cut, which drives S3. Verify the WA
  DOSH threshold in WAC 296-155 Part N.
- The SOR states a **high water table** relative to the slab. No dewatering requirement,
  allowance or unit price appears anywhere in the bid documents.
- At the 3.5 ft base case, about 100 CY of loose spoil goes out through occupied units, and about
  the same volume of bedding and backfill comes back in. Water and sewer service must be restored
  daily by 5:00 PM (SOR General Note 21).

## Construction-phase realism views (team use)

saw-cut and the trench open, bedding and new ABS, the record footing at the trench, Ram Board, poly
dust walls and work lights. A section at the B-103 east lav branch shows the trench against the
1974 footing. Built from the bid set and the record set. Pipe depths, the slab and the protection
layout are assumed and are labelled on every image.

The record footings change the dig. Under every exterior wall the record set shows a strip footing
2'-6" wide, its bottom 3'-4" below the floor. In every unit the lav risers sit over that footing,
so the lav branches run on top of it or beside it. Besides the four tie-in crossings that A-A/P5.1
details, about 27 LF of trench overlaps the footing in plan. At a 3.5 ft tie-in invert, another
44 LF runs below the footing bottom within 1.5 ft of it. At 2.5 ft, nothing does. This is a
screening check, not a structural finding: [table and method](../../products/underslab/REALISM.md#structure-check-structurepy).

## Document conflicts and draft RFIs

SOR General Note 24 reads "All RFI's shall be issued to KEAD within 5 business days of the
deadline for bids". Counted back from Oct 30, that is **Fri Oct 23, 2026**. Treat today as the
cutoff unless KEAD says otherwise. The RFI form is Document 00 63 13.13.

1. **POC inverts and trench depth.** P1.02 keynote 1 says "MATCH EX. = I.E.", and no inverts are
   given. Please provide the existing invert elevation, or depth below finished floor, at each of
   the four 2-way cleanout tie-ins. If they are unknown, confirm that trench depth beyond ___ ft and
   any required protective system will be adjusted by change order. Depth determines whether the
   trench stays at the 2'-0" width in 10/P5.1.
2. **Allowance No. 7 measurement.** Confirm that the 700 SF is measured as plan sawcut area.
   Confirm whether it includes openings at fixture drains, carriers and stack bases, and any extra
   width a trench protective system requires. Confirm that Unit Price No. 7 applies both ways.
3. **Groundwater.** SOR "Initial Investigation" states a high water table relative to the
   Building B slab. Confirm whether dewatering, and disposal of pumped water, is in the base bid.
   If it is, state the basis to assume. If not, provide a unit price or allowance.
4. **B-104 previously replaced run.** P1.02 shows new 4" waste from about grid 3 to riser A at the
   B-104 kitchen: about 30 LF, and about 70 SF of slab in the EHF-2 area. P3.2 labels this run
   "PREVIOUS REPLACED 4" SCHEDULE 40 ABS USED FOR REPAIR". P1.01 clouds the B-104 kitchen with
   "...associated piping shall remain". The SOR mentions a prior repair only in B-101. Confirm
   whether this run is replaced or remains, as the B-101 run does.
5. **Addendum 1 dates.** Document 00 91 13 states "Date of Addendum: October 16, 2024" and "Bid
   Date: October 30, 2024". Confirm that the Invitation to Bid date, October 30, 2026 at 5:00 PM,
   governs.

## Bid clarifications from the 4D sequence

The RFI window closed on Oct 23. These items came out of the unit-by-unit sequence. State them as
clarifications or assumptions in the proposal, or raise them with KEAD if they still take
questions. Each line is marked: **[doc]** is a documented requirement, **[rec]** a
recommendation, **[assume]** an assumption.

1. **Contract time.**
   - **[doc]** The bid form (§1.11, Addendum 1) asks for substantial completion in ___ calendar
     days.
   - **[doc]** Alternates 1 and 2 each ask for the calendar days they add.
   - **[doc]** SOR note 14 says to distinguish calendar days from working days, which exclude
     holidays.
   - **[assume]** The strawman field schedule, one unit at a time, is **81.5 working days, about
     115 calendar days**. It leaves out holidays, submittal review (01 33 00), the SOR mock-up,
     the permit and mobilization.
   - **[rec]** Replace the rates in `schedule.py` with your crew plan before you fill in §1.11.
2. **One ground-level entry.**
   - **[doc]** 00 22 13 §1.14.B: "limit building access to a single entry at ground level."
   - **[doc]** 01 50 00 permits the existing stairs and prohibits the elevators.
   - **[assume]** P2.1 shows two X-hatched cores, at grid C-D and J-K. Read as entries, B-101 and
     B-102 are reached from C-D, and B-103 and B-104 from J-K. No common corridor joins them.
   - **[rec]** State that one entry is in use at a time and that it switches with the unit. If
     KEAD means one entry for the whole job, an interior path to the other pair of units is needed.
3. **Haul path through the planter.**
   - **[doc]** SOR note 3 puts the side-sewer tie-ins in the planter between the garages and
     Building B. SOR Fig 8 puts staging east of the garages.
   - **[doc]** 00 22 13 §1.14.A: the Contractor "shall restore landscaping and/or property damage
     occurring during the Work".
   - **[assume]** Haul runs along that planter and around the north end of the garages, 148–243 ft
     one way. That is about 754 barrow trips out and 629 in, at 4 CF a trip.
   - **[rec]** Price ground protection and landscape restoration along the path. Confirm the path
     with the Association. Consider a power buggy for B-103 and B-104.
4. **Continuous service vs. 5 PM restoration.** This is a document conflict.
   - **[doc]** P2.1 scope note: "Contractor shall maintain continuous sanitary service to occupied
     units and shall provide temporary bypass systems". P1.01 demolition note 3 says the same for
     unaffected units.
   - **[doc]** SOR note 21 asks only that one sink and one toilet work by 5:00 PM daily.
   - **[doc]** P2.1: "the more stringent requirement shall govern as determined by the Engineer of
     Record".
   - **[doc]** The SOR says the upper floors share the stacks. P1.02 shows stack ends in B-102
     (2), B-103 (2) and B-104 (1).
   - **[assume]** A unit's line is open for 10.5–13.5 working days (phases 03–06). Every working
     day of that needs a bypass for the stacks above, plus a nightly reconnection in the unit.
   - **[rec]** Carry the bypass as a priced line, and state the method: pumped or gravity, and
     whether it runs during working hours or only overnight.
   - **[doc]** P1.02, as extracted and reviewed, shows no WC connection in B-102. It is unclear how
     that unit's toilet reaches the under-slab line.
   - **[rec]** Confirm the B-102 toilet's path at the walk.
