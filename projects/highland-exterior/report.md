# Exterior review - 2026-10-16

**Disposition: usable as a first owner presentation study, with unresolved facade detail.** This is not an exact reconstruction of installed conditions.

The southeast and east-entry perspectives and all four orthographic elevations were inspected against the source crops and owner photos. The model captures the general floor stack, stepped wings, upper setback, recurring east/west bays, roof enclosures, dark frames and pale caps. Recessed balcony geometry replaces the original flush facade approximation. A continuous ground mesh removes intersections between separate road/sidewalk surfaces in the presentation views.

Remaining visual differences are material: south openings are narrowed or omitted where the elevation projection conflicts with plan returns; the north central vertical panel/return arrangement is simplified; balcony door divisions and lower patio/retaining walls are not reproduced in detail. West/north site interfaces are approximate. All four source/render pairs are included in the PDF so these differences remain reviewable. Panels are independently fitted, not a same-scale measured overlay.

The floor and grid control assertions pass. The accepted opening inventory is 165, with 26 omitted source candidates and 18 clipped padded recess boundaries recorded separately. Geometry provenance is conservative: complete components are assumed, with documented datums and provisional XY properties recorded separately. `validation.json` records image and GLB packaging checks. Passing those checks is not a dimensional certification.

See [README.md](README.md) for the source inventory, all assumption categories, unavailable-source limitations and reproduction instructions. No facade quantities or construction dimensions are issued from this study. Subsequent accuracy work should reconcile the opening schedule/returns, lower terraces and retaining geometry against additional details or measurements before treating them as resolved.
